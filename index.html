Understanding Railway Reservation Terms

1. Confirmed (CNF): A full sleeping berth is allotted to the passenger at the time of booking.

2. RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation): Two passengers share a side-lower sitting berth; turns into a full sleeping berth if confirmed passengers cancel.

3. Waitlist (WL): No berth is allotted yet. The ticket moves up as Confirmed and RAC passengers cancel their tickets.

4. Quotas (General, Tatkal, Senior Citizen): Dedicated reservation buckets for standard bookings, emergency 1-day-prior travel, and elderly travelers.
