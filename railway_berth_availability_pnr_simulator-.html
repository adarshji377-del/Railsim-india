3. Railway Reservation Terms Guide

Reference guide explaining Indian Railways booking status codes and reservation quotas for passengers.

AVAILABLE / CNF

Confirmed (CNF)

A full dedicated sleeping berth (Lower, Middle, Upper, Side Lower, or Side Upper) is guaranteed for the passenger. Status displays in Green (e.g., Available 42 Seats) when berths are open for immediate allotment.

RAC (Yellow)

Reservation Against Cancellation

The passenger is permitted to board the train. Two RAC passengers share one Side-Lower berth as sitting seats (e.g., RAC 14), and are upgraded to a full sleeping berth as confirmed passengers cancel.

WL / WAITLIST (Red)

Waitlist (WL)

No seat or berth is assigned yet (e.g., WL 28). The ticket advances in the queue as Confirmed and RAC passengers cancel. E-tickets still on the waitlist after chart preparation are automatically cancelled and refunded.

GN / TQ / SS QUOTAS

Reservation Quotas

General (GN): Main seat pool with the highest waitlist confirmation rate.
Tatkal (TQ): Urgent travel quota opening 1 day before departure.
Senior Citizen (SS): Dedicated lower-berth pool for elderly passengers.
